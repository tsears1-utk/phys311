Scene guide

Scene 1 — Collisions

Twenty-four circles collide with one another, the box walls, and an immovable circular obstacle. Change e to compare elastic and inelastic impacts.

Scene 2 — N-body gravity

A central star and four orbiting bodies interact through softened NewtonGravity. Use Velocity Verlet and run the 60-second stability test.

Scene 3 — Spring system

Four masses move under gravity, quadratic drag, and individual spring forces. Their mechanical energy should decrease because drag dissipates energy.
