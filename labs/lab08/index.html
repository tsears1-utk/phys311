What to verify

Part A · vectors and coordinates

Run the Vec2 self-test and confirm every operation passes. In the coordinate demo, the ground is at the bottom and the circle is centered at (2, 3) m with radius 0.3 m.

Part B · projectile

The fading trail follows a 20 m/s projectile launched at 50°. The HUD displays t, x, y, speed, KE, PE, and total energy to three decimals.

Part C · cross-language checks

Run the headless test for 1200 steps at 1/120 s and compare it with validation/python_reference.py. Use the fixed-step checkbox and lag button, then compare Euler–Cromer against Velocity Verlet at DT = 1/20 s.
